The process

From cart to keys, in five steps

Every plan is designed around one thing: your pay cycle. Here's what happens after you place an order.

1. Add items to your cart

Browse the shop and select the furniture, kitchenware or appliances you need. Mix and match categories in a single order.

2. Verify your employment

Submit your civil service ID number and department. Verification typically completes within 48 hours.

3. Choose your plan

Pick a 3, 6 or 12-month term. You'll see the exact monthly amount before you confirm — always in full, upfront.

4. Receive your delivery

Once approved, your order is packed and delivered — to your home, office, or district posting.

5. Pay monthly until settled

Repay via mobile money, bank transfer, or payroll deduction where your employer participates. Miss-free reminders included.

Good to know

Common questions, answered

Who can apply?

Any government or municipal employee with a valid civil service ID and at least six months in post.

What if I pay late?

We'll send reminders three days before each due date. A short grace period applies before any late fee.

Can I settle early?

Yes — pay off the remaining balance any time with no early-settlement penalty.
