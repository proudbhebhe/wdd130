A home shouldn't wait for a bonus

We started HomeGoods because too many civil servants were putting off basic comforts until "someday." We decided someday could be this payday.

Our story

Built by people who understand a government payslip

HomeGoods was founded in 2019 by a small team with family in the civil service — teachers, clerks, and health workers who were quoted furniture prices that assumed a private-sector salary. Bank loans wanted collateral they didn't have. Retail layaway plans stretched too short, or charged too much in the fine print.

So we built something else: a store that prices every item honestly, breaks the total into instalments sized to a real government pay cycle, and delivers wherever the job posts you next — from city offices to rural clinics.

Today, HomeGoods works with civil servants across ten government departments and municipal offices, and we're still run by people who answer their own support line.

What we stand for

Three commitments we don't bend on

Honest pricing

The instalment total is always shown before checkout — never buried, never adjusted after you've committed.

Built for real households

We design plans around monthly government pay dates, not generic 30-day credit cycles.

Quality that lasts

Every item is sourced and tested to outlive its instalment plan by years, not months.
