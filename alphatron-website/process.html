Our Process

Clear expectations, defined scope and a structured journey from first conversation to handover.

From idea to launch

Our workflow is designed to reduce confusion and keep both Alphatron and the client accountable.

1

Discover

We learn about your business, audience, goals, requirements, timeline and the problem you are trying to solve.

2

Plan

We turn discovery into a clear proposed solution, scope, timeline and quotation.

3

Build

Once the project is approved and onboarding requirements are completed, development begins.

4

Review

Client feedback is collected through the agreed review process. Changes outside scope are handled separately.

5

Launch & Handover

The completed project is approved, delivered and handed over using a structured checklist.

6

Care

Clients can continue with Digital Care for agreed maintenance and support.

What happens before we start building?

Discovery comes first. A client request is not automatically a project scope.

  • Client Discovery Form
  • Project Proposal / Quotation
  • Client Agreement
  • Payment and onboarding

Clear scope. Clear expectations. No surprises.

Our processes are designed to make responsibilities, payments, changes and deliverables easier to understand.
