Digital Care

Your website is not finished just because it has launched.

Keep your digital presence cared for.

Digital Care is Alphatron's optional ongoing support service for clients who want help maintaining their website after launch.

  • Website maintenance
  • Agreed content updates
  • Technical support
  • Website health checks
  • Backups where applicable
  • Basic security-related maintenance where included

A simpler way to stay online.

Instead of waiting until something breaks, Digital Care gives your business an ongoing support relationship with clearly defined services.
