Services

Digital services built around what your business actually needs

We don't sell services in isolation. Each one exists to solve a specific business problem — here's what we offer, who it's for, and what it solves.

01

Website Development

Website Development

A professionally designed and built website for your business, organisation, school or event — planned around your goals rather than a generic template.

What it is

A custom-built website covering the pages and features your business genuinely needs.

Who it's for

Businesses, schools and organisations without a website, or ready to build one properly for the first time.

What it solves

The absence of a credible place for customers, parents or partners to find and evaluate you online.

02

Website Redesign

Website Redesign

A rebuild of an existing website that no longer represents your business well, is difficult to update, or doesn't work properly on mobile devices.

What it is

A fresh build informed by what's working and what isn't on your current site.

Who it's for

Businesses with an outdated, slow, or hard-to-manage website.

What it solves

A website that quietly costs you credibility, customers, or time spent fighting an unmanageable system.

03

Landing Pages

Landing Pages

A focused, single-purpose page built around one clear goal — collecting enquiries, promoting an event, or supporting a specific campaign.

What it is

A lean, fast page designed to do one job well, without the overhead of a full website.

Who it's for

Events, product launches, promotions, and time-bound campaigns.

What it solves

The need for a professional, dedicated page quickly, without building an entire site.

04

E-commerce

E-commerce

An online store that lets you list products, take orders, and extend your existing sales process onto the web.

What it is

A store built around your catalogue, sized appropriately to how you actually sell.

Who it's for

Businesses ready to sell online alongside, or instead of, existing channels.

What it solves

Being limited to in-person or word-of-mouth sales when customers expect to buy online.

05

Domain & Hosting

Domain & Hosting

Registering the right domain name for your business and setting up hosting that keeps your site online, fast, and properly configured.

What it is

Domain registration and hosting set-up, handled correctly from the start.

Who it's for

Anyone launching a new site, or moving an existing one to more reliable infrastructure.

What it solves

Downtime, confusing renewals, and websites that are technically difficult to reach.

06

Website Maintenance

Website Maintenance

Ongoing technical upkeep that keeps your website secure, current, and working as intended after launch.

What it is

Scheduled attention to updates, checks, and small fixes, separate from the original build.

Who it's for

Any business that doesn't want to manage technical upkeep themselves.

What it solves

Websites that quietly degrade — broken links, outdated content, unpatched issues — from lack of attention.

07

Digital Care

Digital Care

Our broader ongoing support package — maintenance, small content updates, monitoring and technical support bundled into one relationship.

What it is

An optional, ongoing service that picks up where the original project ends. See full details.

Who it's for

Clients who want their digital presence looked after, not left to age quietly.

What it solves

The gap that opens up once a project closes and there's no one keeping an eye on things.

08

Other Digital Solutions

Other Digital Solutions

Where a business problem calls for something beyond a standard website — a basic booking form, a simple automation, or another practical digital tool — we'll discuss whether it's something we can build.

What it is

Scoped case by case, once we understand the specific problem.

Who it's for

Businesses with a specific operational need that a standard website doesn't cover.

What it solves

Varies by project — we'll only propose this where it's genuinely the right fit.
