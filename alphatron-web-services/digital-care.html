Digital Care

Launching your website is not the end of the relationship.

A website that's never touched again slowly falls behind — outdated content, unpatched software, small problems no one noticed. Digital Care is our answer to that: ongoing attention, on a schedule that suits you.

What's included

What Digital Care covers

Digital Care is built from a defined set of services. We won't promise anything beyond what's listed here — if you need something outside this scope, we'll discuss it directly.

  • Website maintenance
  • Updates
  • Basic security monitoring
  • Backups, where applicable
  • Small content updates
  • Technical support
  • Website health checks
  • Assistance with ongoing digital needs

How it works

Optional, separate, and clearly scoped

It's optional

Digital Care is not automatically bundled into every website project. You decide whether you want it, and when.

It's separate from the build

The original project has its own defined scope and its own close. Digital Care is a distinct, ongoing arrangement that starts after that.

It's clearly defined

You'll know what's included before you commit — no vague promises of "full support" with undefined limits.

It scales to you

A small brochure site and a growing e-commerce store don't need the same level of attention. We scope Digital Care to fit.

Who it's for

Businesses that want their site looked after

Digital Care makes the most sense if you don't have the time, interest, or in-house technical capacity to manage your website yourself — but still want it to stay secure, current and working properly.

Want to know what Digital Care would look like for your site?

Get in touch and we'll talk through what makes sense for your website and how it's currently maintained.
