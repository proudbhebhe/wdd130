Process

A structured client journey, start to finish

We use the same six-stage process for every project, scaled to the size of the work. It exists so that nothing gets built before it's understood, and nothing gets forgotten after it launches.

01

Discover

Understand your business, audience, goals and problems.

02

Plan

Recommend a solution and define scope.

03

Build

Design and develop the agreed solution.

04

Review

Controlled review and revision.

05

Launch

Professional delivery of the completed project.

06

Care

Optional ongoing support.

01

Discover

Before we propose anything, we take the time to understand what we'd actually be building and why. This stage usually covers a short conversation or questionnaire about:

  • Your business
  • Target audience
  • The problem you're solving
  • Desired outcome
  • Requirements
  • Timeline
  • Budget
  • Existing assets
02

Plan

Based on what we've learned, we recommend an appropriate solution — not necessarily the most complex one — and put together a clear scope covering what's included, the approximate timeline, and the cost. You'll know what you're agreeing to before any work begins.

03

Build

We design and develop the agreed solution. If anything comes up that falls outside the original scope, it's handled through a straightforward change request rather than being quietly added in or quietly ignored.

04

Review

You review the work against what was agreed, and we work through a defined revision process together. This keeps feedback focused and the project moving toward completion, rather than open-ended.

05

Launch

The completed project is delivered professionally, with a proper handover — access details, a walkthrough of what's been built, and clarity on what happens next.

06

Care

The original project is formally closed once it's launched — it doesn't just trail off. If you'd like ongoing support after that, Digital Care picks up separately, on its own clear terms.
